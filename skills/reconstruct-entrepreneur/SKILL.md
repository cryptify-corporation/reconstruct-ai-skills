# Research a reason to build the product

Use public user feedback to choose improvements for the intended audience. Reconstruct captures help explain an interface; they do not establish demand, market size or willingness to pay.

## Bound the research

Identify the reference products, audience and decision the research should support. Start with a bounded sample of recent feedback and expand only when evidence is thin or conflicting. Do not automatically collect 100+ reviews for a narrow question. Track sources, dates, duplicate or syndicated feedback and the sampling method.

Read current review pages, public request boards, discussions, pricing and changelogs through available authorized tools. Respect access restrictions and use official feeds/APIs where appropriate. Every retained review needs a real source URL and date when available. If a source is inaccessible, record the gap rather than inventing its contents.

Use [the review sheet](assets/reviews.csv) for substantial research. Quotes must be exact, linked and within applicable quotation limits; mark paraphrases as summaries. A URL field is not evidence that a quotation has been verified. Do not turn competitors' reviewers into testimonials for the new product.

## Interpret

Separate complaints, feature requests and underserved jobs. Count unique observations and sources, retain contradictory feedback and compare with the latest product behavior. Regex or keyword grouping is only an organizing aid: inspect negation, context and unmatched feedback. A handful of angry comments is not a representative market estimate.

For each important opportunity identify the affected audience, evidence strength, current workaround, proposed improvement, implementation cost and how it could be validated. Repeated complaints do not prove payment intent; propose interviews or a small experiment when that matters to the decision.

## Deliver

Give the sample size, source mix, limitations, ranked opportunities and a small number of positioning options. Tie each proposed feature to its evidence and mark weak themes accordingly. Recommend the next validation step, not an automatic full rebuild, new pricing system or launch campaign. Keep research independent of the core reconstruction workflow unless the user requested both.
