# Prepare the product launch

Build launch materials around the actual product, intended audience and supported improvements. This workflow prepares reviewable materials; publishing or messaging people requires the user's authorization.

## Check what is real

Read the implementation and verification results before promising capabilities. Separate available features, beta limitations and roadmap ideas. Use existing positioning and brand work if available, without forcing unrelated stages. Do not invent testimonials, user counts, customer logos, ratings, performance claims or review quotes.

## Create requested materials

For a landing page, connect the audience's problem, the demonstrated workflow, relevant features and a clear action. Use screenshots of the actual product. Follow the project's existing frontend and publishing conventions; create a site or page only when requested.

For pricing, read current first-party competitor pricing with dates and URLs. Propose a model using the product's costs, usage units and intended audience. Distinguish a pricing hypothesis from validated willingness to pay. Do not infer sustainable pricing simply by undercutting a competitor or assume three tiers and annual discounts are always appropriate.

For mobile store listings, check current official field and asset requirements for the requested store and locale. Prepare only applicable metadata, screenshots, support/privacy links and review notes. Static character limits may be outdated. Never imply guaranteed store acceptance.

For a beta or launch plan, identify the feedback to collect, success criteria, instrumentation, support and readiness dependencies. Draft outreach when requested, but do not send email, post to communities or publish listings without authorization.

## Deliver

Provide the requested copy, pricing proposal or launch checklist with sources, assumptions and missing dependencies. Keep promises consistent with verified functionality. If launch uncovers an implementation gap, record the gap and propose a bounded fix instead of silently adding it to the product or triggering production deployment.
